# Your Rights

Last updated: 8 October 2026

You can use most of these rights directly in the app. For the rest, write to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]. We answer within one month (45 days for California requests, extendable once where the law allows and we tell you why). Exercising a right is free. We may ask for proof that the account is yours, and we may refuse or charge a reasonable fee only for requests that are clearly unfounded or excessive.

## In the app

| You want to | Do this |

## Rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

- Information (Arts. 13 and 14): this documentation.
- Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), portability (Art. 20).
- Object (Art. 21) to processing based on legitimate interests; we will stop unless we show compelling grounds.
- Withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)), where processing rests on consent, such as microphone access that you can revoke in your device settings.
- Not to be subject to purely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effect (Art. 22). We do not make any.
- Complain to a supervisory authority: in your country of residence, workplace, or where the issue arose. The list is at edpb.europa.eu. In the UK: the ICO (ico.org.uk).

## Switzerland

Rights to information, access, correction, deletion, objection and portability under the FADP; complaints to the FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch).

## California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

You may ask to know what we collect and how we use it, to access and obtain a portable copy, to delete, to correct, and to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of; we also honour Global Privacy Control. We do not discriminate against you for using your rights. You can use an authorised agent. If we decline, you can appeal by replying to our decision; in some states you may then contact the state attorney general.

## Canada, Brazil, India, Australia, Japan, South Korea, South Africa

You have access, correction and deletion rights, and the right to complain to the regulator (OPC and provincial bodies in Canada; ANPD in Brazil under LGPD Art. 18; the Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act; the OAIC in Australia; the PPC in Japan; the PIPC in Korea; the Information Regulator in South Africa).
