# Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 8 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to learn with Sprichst. Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA and EN 301 549, in line with the European Accessibility Act and similar laws. We have not yet had a full external audit, so we do not claim conformance. [[CONFIRM_IF_MICRO_ENTERPRISE_EXEMPTION_APPLIES]]

## What is in place

- Light, dark or your device's own appearance (Account → Appearance). Every text and control colour pair is checked against WCAG AA contrast in both, by automated tests.
- Buttons and icon buttons have a minimum touch target of 48 by 48 pixels.
- Text follows your device's text-size setting.
- When your device asks to reduce motion, animations (headings, option tiles, voice mode) become instant and the loading arc stops, with words beside it.
- Many controls carry labels or tooltips for screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack).
- Every spoken reply is also shown as text.

## Known limits

- Not every control has been checked with a screen reader; some labels may be missing or unclear.
- Some games depend on quick reactions or on seeing the screen and have no alternative yet.
- The web version draws the interface on a canvas, which exposes less structure to assistive technology than a native page.
- AI-generated text can be long or oddly formatted.

## Tell us

Report barriers at [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] or Account → Contact developer. We reply within 14 days. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can contact the competent market surveillance authority of your country; in Germany that is the Marktüberwachungsstelle der Länder für die Barrierefreiheit von Produkten und Dienstleistungen (MSBS).
