# Voice

## Hands-free voice mode

Coach → the waveform button (or "Start voice conversation"). The tutor listens, notices when you have finished, answers aloud, and listens again. Nothing needs pressing between turns.

How it decides you have finished (`SpeechEndpointer`, pure Dart):

1. For the first 0.4 s it measures the background loudness of the room.
2. Speech is sound clearly above that level for at least 0.25 s, so a cough or a door is ignored.
3. Your turn ends after a pause. The pause is adjustable in Account → AI & voice → Hands-free voice mode (Short 0.8 s, Normal 1.2 s, Long 1.8 s). Choose Long if you like to think while you speak.
4. Turns are capped at 30 s.

What happens next (`VoiceSession`): the clip is transcribed, the tutor answers, the reply is spoken, then the microphone opens again. It is half-duplex on purpose: the microphone is closed while the tutor speaks, so it cannot hear itself. Tap skip to cut a reply short.

Safeguards:

- Two waits with no speech, or three failures in a row, pause listening with a plain message rather than looping.
- Captions that speech recognition invents out of silence ("Untertitel …") are ignored.
- A voice playback failure keeps the written reply and keeps listening.
- Listening pauses when the app leaves the foreground, and the microphone is released.
- Space pauses or resumes; Escape ends.

## Voices

Speech is generated by the gateway (`POST /v1/speak`) with a voice id from `GET /v1/voices`. Open-source German voices from [rhasspy/piper-voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices), run with [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl):

| Voice | Quality | Training data licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thorsten (`de_DE-thorsten-medium`, default) | medium | CC0 |
| Thorsten, high quality | high | CC0 |
| Thorsten, expressive | medium | CC0 |
| Kerstin | low | CC0 |
| Ramona | low | M-AILABS open data |
| Karlsson | low | M-AILABS open data |
| Eva | x_low | M-AILABS open data |

Left out on purpose: **Pavoque**, whose data is CC BY-NC-SA (non-commercial). Other open engines were considered and not used: Kokoro has no German voice, and XTTS and Meta MMS-TTS carry non-commercial licences.

Notes:

- The Piper engine is GPL-3.0. The gateway runs it as a server-side service and does not distribute it with the app.
- Check each model's `MODEL_CARD` before shipping a voice commercially. For the M-AILABS voices the model card only says "See URL", so read the terms on the M-AILABS dataset page yourself.
- An uninstalled or unknown voice falls back to the best working voice, and the response carries `X-Voice-Fallback: true`. Older profiles that stored a system voice name such as "Anna" keep working.
- On macOS the built-in German voices are listed too and always work.
- Speed is the learner's words-per-minute setting (120 to 260), mapped to Piper's `length_scale`.
- Voice quality has not been listened to in this repository's tests, which use a fake engine. Install a voice and use the preview button to judge it.
