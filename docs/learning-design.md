# Learning design

Why Sprichst teaches the way it does.

## What it models

- **Goethe-Zertifikat (A1–C2) and TestDaF (B2–C1)** test four modules: Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, Sprechen. Exercises carry an `examPart` such as "Lesen Teil 1" and follow the real task shapes: reading questions over a passage (Richtig/Falsch, multiple choice), listening items, Lückentext, writing to content points (Leitpunkte) with the right register (du/Sie), speaking prompts, and a graph description (TestDaF).
- The Goethe-Zertifikat is passed **per module at 60%**, so Sprichst shows 60% as the line. TestDaF reports TDN levels that depend on each test set, so for TestDaF the readiness figures are explicitly a guide.
- Sprichst is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Goethe-Institut or TestDaF-Institut. The tasks are original practice material in the same style.

## What it uses from learning science

| Principle | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Spaced retrieval | Flashcards and review items return at growing gaps; a forgotten card returns in minutes. |
| Retrieval over recognition | Cards flip to meaning → German once well known; typed and built answers are weighted into the plan when recognition runs ahead of recall. |
| Interleaving | Practice mixes skills and kinds instead of drilling one. |
| Desirable difficulty | Difficulty per skill follows accuracy. |
| Gender with the noun | Nouns are learned with their article, and cards hide it on the front. |
| Comprehensible input | Reading and listening tasks sit at or below the learner's level; transcripts are available. |

## What it deliberately does not do

**Learning styles (VARK).** The idea that people learn best in their "visual", "auditory" or "kinesthetic" style is not supported by controlled studies. Sprichst adapts to **what a learner actually does**: how accurately they recognise vs. recall, which exercise kinds they struggle with, and which skills lag. The insights shown on the Progress screen come from those measurements only.

## Games

Games are practice, not decoration: each trains one thing (articles, word meanings, spelling, five-letter vocabulary, conversational replies), feeds the same skill statistics, and is short. Combos reward streaks; wrong answers cost the bonus, not the points already earned. Nothing is lost for stopping, and there are no lives or penalties for missing a day.
